Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard
Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyse Adidas sales performance across time, regions, product categories, and retail partners.
The dashboard brings key sales metrics and comparisons into one view, helping users explore business performance and identify sales trends.
Dashboard Preview

Add your dashboard screenshot to this repository and name it dashboard.png, or update the image path above to match your file name.

Key Metrics
The dashboard displays these headline KPIs:
- Total Sales: $859M
- Operating Profit: $317M
- Units Sold: 2M
- Price per Unit: $45
- Operating Margin: 42%
Values are displayed as shown in the dashboard and may be rounded.
Dashboard Features
- Monthly Sales Trend: Tracks sales across the months to highlight stronger and weaker periods.
- Sales by Region: Compares sales across West, Northeast, Southeast, South, and Midwest.
- Geographical Sales Map: Displays sales by state.
- Sales by Product: Compares revenue across product categories.
- Sales by Retailer: Compares sales contributions from retail partners.
- Interactive Filters: Region and invoice-date slicers allow users to explore a selected segment or time period.
Key Insights
Based on the dashboard:
- The West region has the highest sales, at approximately $270M (30%).
- Men's Street Footwear is the leading product category, at approximately $199M.
- West Gear is the top retailer, at approximately $233M.
- Monthly sales peak in July at approximately $95M, while December is the lowest month shown, at approximately $51M.
These are descriptive findings from the displayed dashboard; they do not by themselves explain why the differences occurred.

How to Explore
1. Download the .pbix file from this repository.
2. Open it in Power BI Desktop.
3. Use the region and invoice-date slicers to filter the report.
4. Explore the charts to compare monthly sales, regions, products, and retailers.
Notes
